The Fine Print, Minus the Fine Print

Can I get a refund?

Full refunds until September 1, 2025. After that, 50% until September 20, then no refunds — but you can always transfer.

Can I transfer my badge?

Yes. Transfers are free and open until September 28; log into your order and reassign the badge name.

Are badges mailed?

All badges are digital. Your QR code lands in your inbox 48 hours before the con.

Can I upgrade my tier?

Yes — pay the price difference any time before September 20.

What if I'm under 18?

Fans 13 and under need a guardian with a badge; 14–17 can attend solo with a signed waiver.
